1. Transmitter Pipeline

UTF-8 payload padded and encoded via RS(48,40). Mapped to 96 16-APSK symbols driving continuous wave amplitude and phase modulation.

2. Receiver Pipeline

Sub-pixel weighted centroids locate wave coordinates. 24-point normalized line slices are classified via 1D CNN on ONNX Runtime Web.

3. Sub-Pixel Centroid + 1D CNN

Replaces thresholding with continuous sub-pixel energy localization for blur and contrast invariance.

Weighted Centroid Sub-pixel skeleton tracking; invariant to stroke width, antialiasing bleed, and optical lighting.
1D CNN Classifier Resilient to optical lens curvature, perspective skew, and noise harmonics.
